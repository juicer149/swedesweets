Get to know SwedeSweets

SwedeSweets was founded by two Franco-Swedish cousins, Marco and Milo, raised between the landscapes of Chamonix and the traditions of Sweden.

Growing up, one tradition stood out above all: lösgodis — the Nordic pick & mix candy experience. More than just sweets, it’s a ritual: choosing, mixing, and creating your own perfect selection.

SwedeSweets was created to bring that experience to stores in France. We carefully source authentic Swedish confectionery and offer a wide selection, from sweet and sour to chocolate and liquorice.

Our goal is simple: help stores offer something unique, high-quality, and genuinely Swedish — without complexity.

Welcome to SwedeSweets.

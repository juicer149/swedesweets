Get to know SwedeSweets

A modern take on a Swedish tradition.

SwedeSweets was founded by two Franco-Swedish cousins, Marco and Milo, raised between the landscapes of Chamonix and the traditions of Sweden.

Growing up, one tradition stood out above all: lösgodis — the Nordic pick & mix candy experience.

More than just sweets, it’s a ritual: choosing, mixing, and creating your own perfect selection.

Our mission

SwedeSweets was created to bring that experience to stores in France.

We carefully source authentic Swedish confectionery and offer a wide selection, from sweet and sour to chocolate and liquorice.

Our goal is simple: help stores offer something unique, high-quality, and genuinely Swedish — without complexity.

Built for stores

We work closely with our partners to make Swedish pick & mix easy to offer, easy to manage, and easy to sell.

From product selection to delivery, everything is designed to be simple.

Product price

Every variant sells at this price, unless its price tag is on. The EUR and SEK tabs only change which currency you see; both are saved.

A variant

Label: what tells it apart from the others (S, M, 450 g). Weight: grams per unit. The weight is fixed once the variant has stock or orders.

Order

The order here is the order the sizes are offered in: in the shop and in the business catalog, S before M before L if that is how they stand here. When an order only names the product, the first variant that is sold is the one taken.

Move a variant with its arrows, or drag it by the dots between them.

Sales channels

  • Retail: sold in the shop. It then needs a retail price.
  • Business: sold to business customers.
  • Neither: paused. The variant is kept, but not sold anywhere.

Own price (the tag)

Green: the variant has prices of its own, in the yellow tab under it, one field per channel it is sold in. A field left empty is the product price (shown faintly in it).

Deleting

The bin deletes a variant that has never been used. Once it has had stock, carts or orders it stays: switch both its channels off to pause it.

Saving

Nothing changes until Update product. Changing a price that is already set asks for your password; a first price does not. Back turns into Cancel once something has changed: Cancel leaves without saving.

{% comment %} What the Product section of the product form means (its help dialog, includes/ui/help.html). {% endcomment %}

Catalog ID

The product's number in the customer catalog (optional). Two products cannot share one.

SKU

The product's stock code, made when it was added: from its catalog ID (SS-607), or without one from brand, name and weight. It does not change afterwards. The first variant has the product's SKU; each one added after it the product's plus its label (SS-607-M).

Brand and name

The brand, and the product's own name: its Swedish name, kept as it is. What the shop calls it in each language is its shop text (its own page). The category and the picture are the same in every language: set them here.

Manufacturer

Who makes it (optional), when that is not the brand.

Status

Active: the product can be sold, in the channels its variants are in. Switched off it is not sold anywhere, but kept with all its history.

Attributes

Vegan shows as a tag in the shop and the catalog.
